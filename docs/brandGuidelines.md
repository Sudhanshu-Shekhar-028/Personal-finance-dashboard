# JW Solutionss Brand Guidelines

## Brand Nase

*2IM Solutionssex

Always written with two “s" at the end.  
Never written as “JN Solutions" (single s).

### #8 Acceptable Usage
- JM Solutions  
- JM SOLUTIONS (all caps)

### #48 Unacceptable Usage
- JM Solutions (single s)  
- Jn Solutions (lowercase)  
- jm solutions (all lowercase)  
- IMSolutionss (no space)

---

### Logo Usage

### Clear Space
Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the “J* in the logo on all sides.

- Digital: 120px width minimum  
- Print: 1 inch / 25mm width minimum

### Logo Don'ts
- Do not stretch or distort the logo  
- Do not rotate the logo  
- Do not change the logo colors outside of approved variations  
- Do not add effects (shadows, gradients, outlines)  
- Do not place the logo on busy backgrounds

### Typography

### Primary Font
**Inter** (or SF Pro, Helvetica Neue as fallbacks)

### Font Weights
- **Bold (700)**: Headings, emphasis  
- **Semi-Bold (600)**: Subheadings, labels  
- **Regular (488)**: Body text, paragraphs  
- **Light (300)**: Captions, secondary text (use sparingly)

### Font Sizes
- H1: 32px / 2rem  
- H2: 24px / 1.5rem  
- H3: 20px / 1.25rem  
- Body: 16px / lrem  
- Small: 14px / 0.875ren  
- Caption: 12px / 0.75rem

### Line Height
- Headings: 1.2  
- Body: 1.5

## UI Elements

### Buttons

**Primary Buttons**
- Background: JM Dark Blue (#2E3A8C)  
- Text: White (#FFFFFF)  
- Border Radius: 8px  
- Hover: JM Light Blue (#4A5FD9)

**Secondary Buttons**
- Background: Transparent  
- Border: 2px solid JM Dark Blue (#2E3A8C)  
- Text: JM Dark Blue (#2E3A8C)  
- Hover: Light blue background tint

---

### Cards
- Background: White (#FFFFFF)  
- Border Radius: 12px  
- Shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)

---

### Input Fields
- Border: 1px solid #E6E0ED  
- Border Radius: 8px  
- Focus Border: JM Dark Blue (#2E3ABC)

---

## Iconography
- Style: Outlined or filled, consistent stroke width  
- Size: 24px standard, 20px small, 32px large  
- Color: Match text color or JM Dark Blue for emphasis

## Tone of Voice
- **Professional**: Clear, trustworthy, knowledgeable  
- **Approachable**: Friendly, helpful, not overly formal  
- **Concise**: Direct, no jargon, easy to understand
